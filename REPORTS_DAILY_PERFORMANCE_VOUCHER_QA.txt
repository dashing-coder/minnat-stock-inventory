M/S Afrin Traders ERP — Daily Performance + Voucher Print QA
Date: 2026-10-22

Implemented features
1. Daily Performance report
   Route: /reports/daily-performance
   Includes: Sales, Purchases, Customer Collections, Supplier Payments, Operating Expenses,
   Sales Returns, Purchase Returns, Cash In, Cash Out, Sales Due, Purchase Due, Accounting Income,
   Accounting Expense and Net Profit for the selected day.

2. Voucher Print
   Route: /reports/vouchers
   Print route: /reports/vouchers/{journal}/print
   Includes: posted voucher register, date/type/text search, A4 printable accounting voucher,
   automatic voucher title based on transaction type, debit/credit lines and totals.
   Print Voucher shortcuts were also added to Journal list/detail pages.

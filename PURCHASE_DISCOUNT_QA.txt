ACCOUNTING FIX
Previous PurchaseService grand total did not subtract item-level discounts.
New formula:
  Subtotal Before Discount
- Item Discount
- Invoice Discount
+ Item Tax
+ Invoice Tax
+ Shipping
+ Other Cost
= Purchase Grand Total

EDIT SAFETY
purchase.tax stores invoice tax + item tax. The Purchase edit form now derives and displays only invoice-level tax, preventing item tax from being counted twice when a draft is edited.

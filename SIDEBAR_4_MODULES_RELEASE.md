# Sidebar 4-Module Reorganization

The ERP sidebar has been reorganized into four primary collapsible modules while preserving the existing role/permission checks.

## Top level
- Dashboard (standalone home link)
- Bills
- Products
- Reports
- Settings

## Bills
Contains Sales, POS, Sales Returns, Quotations, Purchases, Purchase Returns, Customer Receipts, Supplier Payments, Customers, Suppliers, Expenses, Payroll, Chart of Accounts, Loan Management, and Journal Entries.

## Products
Contains Products, Categories, Brands, Units, Current Stock, Reorder Suggestions, Damaged Goods, Batch Management, Expiry Alerts, Stock Ledger, Stock Adjustments, Stock Transfers, and Warehouses.

## Reports
Contains All Reports, Sales Report, Purchase Report, Cashbook, Profit & Loss, Customer Report, Supplier Report, Expense Report, Stock Valuation, and Excess Stock.

## Settings
Contains Customer Groups, Supplier Groups, Seller Accounts, Users, Roles & Permissions, Notifications, Audit Log, Company Settings, and Data Backups.

The active module opens automatically. Bootstrap accordion behavior keeps the sidebar compact by closing another module when a new one is opened.

No database changes are required.
